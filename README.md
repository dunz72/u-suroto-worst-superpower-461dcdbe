My entry for the Worst Superpower bounty invents the most useless superpower imaginable in a single line: the power to recolour any traffic cone — but only to a slightly different shade of orange. It is a power with a perfectly pointless limitation: no crime stopped, no life improved, no cone ever meaningfully changed, just one hero out there doing the world's most invisible redecoration, one almost-identical orange at a time.
